# BTS "Body to Body" - Scene Plan

## Infinite Zoom Flow
Mountains → zoom into crater lake → Stadium → zoom into stage → Members performing

**Visual continuity:** 천지 crater lake → stadium bowl (shape), dancheong border → LED wall pattern (tradition → modern), moon → backlight god-ray (light), ocean waves → crowd waves → LED scroll (motion)

---

## Scene 1: Night / Moonlit Baekdu Mountain (DONE)
**Theme:** Korean traditional art, Arirang, night sky
**Palette:** Deep indigo, obangsaek colors, gold accents
**Components:** 15 layers (stars, moon, clouds, Baekdu with 천지, mountains, cranes, blossoms, waves, ribbons, dancheong border, typography, hanji texture)

---

## Scene 2: Stadium Concert
**Theme:** Massive BTS concert arena from the nosebleeds, looking down at the stage
**Palette:** Concert purples, magentas, cyans on deep darkness
**Components:** 11 layers
- StadiumBackground — dark sky, stars, city lights above open roof
- StadiumStructure — roof arch, 3 seating tiers, support beams, railings, aisles, exit signs, concourse openings
- Catwalk — T-shaped runway extending into crowd (BTS concert signature), B-stage circle, LED edge lighting
- CrowdLayer — ~300 silhouette figures (4 pose variants) with wave-jump animation across 5 sections
- ArmyBombField — ~350 glowing purple/blue dots (the iconic ARMY ocean), coordinated twinkling
- StadiumStage — main platform, LED screens, jumbotrons, stage lip LED strip, risers, tiny member previews
- SoundRig — hanging speaker arrays, lighting trusses, moving head lights, cable lines
- SpotlightBeams — 6 upward stage beams + 3 overhead sweeping spots
- StadiumLighting — color wash overlay, lens flares, haze/fog bands, strobe flashes
- ConfettiLayer — 80 falling confetti pieces + streamer strips
- StadiumText — "BODY TO BODY" on jumbotrons

---

## Scene 3: BTS Members on Stage
**Theme:** Close-up stage with 7 member silhouettes performing, dramatic lighting
**Palette:** Stage purples/blues with pyro oranges, backlight whites
**Components:** 12 layers
- StageFloor — reflective gradient surface, LED edge strip, floor tape marks
- LEDWall — massive screen with scrolling dancheong-inspired geometric patterns (echoes Scene 1)
- StageBacklight — god-ray radial gradient + ray lines behind members
- StageEquipment — monitor speakers, mic stands, camera boom silhouette, in-ear packs
- BackupDancers — 4-6 smaller flanking silhouettes
- MemberReflections — flipped/faded/blurred floor reflections
- MemberSilhouettes — 7 unique dance pose silhouettes in V-formation with rim lighting (CENTER PIECE)
- PyroEffects — 6 flame columns (3 per side) with inner/outer flames, ember particles, base glow
- MemberSpotlights — 7 individual conic spots from above + floor light pools
- LaserBeams — 8-10 colored lasers sweeping, 2 crossing in X-pattern
- SparkParticles — rising sparks from pyro + floating dust + floor smoke wisps
- StageText — "BODY TO BODY" on LED wall
